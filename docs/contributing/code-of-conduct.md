# Code of Conduct

> Our standards for a welcoming, productive community.

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Be kind and respectful.** Treat others as you want to be treated.
- **Be precise.** Communicate clearly and avoid ambiguity.
- **Assume good faith.** Most misunderstandings come from miscommunication, not malice.
- **Be patient.** Not everyone has the same background or available time.
- **Accept constructive feedback.** We're all here to learn and improve.
- **Focus on the work.** Critique ideas, not people.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment** of any kind — sexual, personal, or professional.
- **Trolling, insults, or derogatory comments.**
- **Public or private harassment.**
- **Publishing others' private information** without permission.
- **Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.**
- **Other conduct** that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, and any other communication channel related to the project. It also applies to behavior outside project spaces when it affects the community (e.g., one-on-one harassment of a community member).

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this code of conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it by contacting a project maintainer. Reports will be handled confidentially.

For security vulnerabilities, do not open public issues — see [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
